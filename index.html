What exactly is an AI Version of You?

It is an AI experience built around your approved health and wellness expertise. It explains your methods, answers common questions, understands what a person needs, and connects them with a suitable digital program. It makes your knowledge more accessible without adding another one-to-one appointment.

What does Kodara build for me?

Our team extracts and organizes your expertise, builds the AI Version of You, creates the $500-$2,000 digital programs, and produces the website, branding, AI experience, and launch. You provide the knowledge, feedback, testing, and approvals. We handle the build.

Is this an online course or group coaching program?

No. This is not an online course, group coaching program, or agency that only hands over leads. Kodara builds an interactive AI business around your expertise so people can receive relevant guidance without another one-to-one appointment or weekly group call from you.

Can AI safely represent my expertise?

Your AI is built from the knowledge and materials you approve. You test and audit the experience before launch, and nothing goes live under your name until you approve it. In a Mayo Clinic study of 30 patients, every participant considered an AI model of their surgeon just as trustworthy as the surgeon, and all 30 said they would recommend the experience.

Do I need a large social-media following?

No. Built from more than 100,000 healthcare leads, Kodara’s agentic client-discovery model analyzes live search and demand signals to identify people likely to want your expertise. You do not need to become a full-time content creator.

How much time will this require from me?

Plan on about one hour per week. We use that time for focused knowledge-extraction interviews, feedback, and approvals. You can also give us existing videos, documents, frameworks, client questions, and other materials that already capture how you work.

What happens before anything goes live?

You review the digital programs, website, branding, and AI experience. You test how the AI answers questions, audit the important paths, and approve the final experience. We do not launch an unreviewed version under your name.

How does the guarantee work?

For qualifying clients, within the first 30 days on the launch timeline, your first AI Version will be completed and launched and your first 10 beta users will be onboarded, or you receive a full refund. Your signed agreement controls eligibility, timing, definitions, and the exact remedy.

# Kodara landing page

This context describes the page sections that explain and sell Kodara's done-for-you AI sales department.

## Language

**Primary demo**:
The single main visual explanation placed immediately below the authority logos. It uses a split layout with page-owned sales copy on the left and the interactive demo on the right, and it replaces the previous four-demo sequence.
_Avoid_: Demo grid, four-demo section

**AI Brain**:
The shared knowledge layer trained on the client's offers, buyers, voice, qualification rules, and business knowledge. It gives every AI salesperson the context to represent the business accurately.
_Avoid_: Generic chatbot, knowledge bot

**AI Triagers**:
The AI sales roles that speak with cold prospects and guide suitable buyers toward the low-cost personalized assessment.
_Avoid_: Chatbots, lead bots

**AI Salespeople**:
The AI sales roles that route qualified buyers toward the client's highest-level service, present smaller offers to other buyers, and continue follow-up.
_Avoid_: Bots, agents

**Founder letter**:
The founder-written long-form sales argument that follows the primary demo and moves the reader from the lead-conversion problem to Kodara's mechanism and offer.
_Avoid_: Blog post, company letter

**Mechanism features**:
The first three-part visual section explaining how the AI sales department works.
_Avoid_: First feature cards, generic features

**Founder highlight**:
The portrait-and-bio section that establishes Lucas's relevant credibility and motivation for building Kodara.
_Avoid_: About us, founder résumé

**Proof section**:
The existing three video testimonials and eight stat-or-quote testimonial cards, reorganized to lead with one featured story and two supporting videos.
_Avoid_: Client logo wall, generic social proof
